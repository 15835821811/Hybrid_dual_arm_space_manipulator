## Non-support and concrete concerns

1. **Risk-cohort coverage limitation:** data.py selects distinct families across random and risk cohorts. Once the three obstacle families enter the random cohort, the risk cohort cannot include their more difficult rows. Original 16/16 success therefore cannot establish coverage down to the 0.225 mm model-clearance case. The separately frozen three-case supplement is appropriate and must retain failures without replacement. Its outcomes remain unknown here.
2. **Run-relative provenance flag is misleading:** training_input_audit.json retains `new_in_this_run=true` on twelve pre-existing complex_train_val_001 records (six TRAIN, six VAL); current expansion entries omit this field. data.py combined() copies the old index. This is a metadata semantics defect, not evidence of twelve new expansion rows or VAL training leakage. Use explicit current-run source paths/IDs or an append-only run-relative provenance mapping when reporting counts. The independently checked 744 expansion TRAIN count is unaffected. Do not rewrite live inputs to repair the historical flag.
3. The saved coverage snapshot lists ten EXECUTION_VERIFIED rows, while the later trace audit has sixteen. These are different stage snapshots; preserve their timestamps/scope rather than presenting them as a single simultaneous state.
4. Model data, codec rank/dimension (102 versus old 119), and training updates (3,600 versus old 1,800) change together. A future old/new comparison cannot isolate a data-only causal effect. The model is not obstacle-conditioned; obstacle safety must be attributed to the full pipeline where warranted.
5. TRAIN mechanism evidence (9/9 guided versus 8/9 goal-only and one repaired penetration) can support a scoped guidance example, not neural superiority, broad reliability or statistical generalization. Distinct IDs/root groups are not independent trials. Denoising RMSE is not physical success.
6. No completed DEV verdict, boundary verdict, pilot result, final TEST result, whole-library dynamic validity, useful multimodality, duration generalization, or relative performance advantage follows from this stage.
