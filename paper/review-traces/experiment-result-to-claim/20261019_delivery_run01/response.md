## What the results do not support

C2 is not established: all methods tie on candidate and execution success, while the new Diffusion median maximum-arm position error is 1.0328 mm versus 0.2716 mm for structured spline and 0.1357 mm for conditional mean. Compared with old Diffusion's 1.0460 mm median, the new model has worse worst-case position and orientation errors (3.6215 versus 2.8959 mm; 0.6907 versus 0.2953 degrees). Recorded Diffusion query/time totals are also larger than those baselines. No stable superiority follows from these mixed outcomes, and shared physical correction prevents attribution of end-to-end success to the learned proposal alone.

The 12 tasks are original DEV4 plus already-used eight development tasks, with three sampling seeds and one training run. They do not establish untouched-family generalization, large-scale coverage, statistical independence of 180 successes, robust performance across training seeds, or hardware performance. M3 did not remeasure bare proposals. Teacher-local v+0.05G is not exact nonlinear endpoint-gradient supervision; old D=0.914089 and historical blocked DEV remain valid historical failures. Finite sampled geometry coverage is not an arbitrary continuous-time collision proof. Only the selected subset is execution verified.
